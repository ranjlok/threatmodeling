Purpose

Manual threat modeling is thorough but slow — often days of workshops for a single system. A-SDF's tool takes the same asset-first discipline and runs it through AI: it identifies what's being protected, checks the design against known anti-patterns, and produces a threat model with categorized threats and a qualitative risk score, all traceable back to specific assets.

Scope

Three modeling domains, covering how systems are actually built today.

application

Application design

Threat model a service or feature from its architecture and data flows.

integration

Integration design

Model multi-system flows — APIs, middleware, third-party connections.

cloud · in development

Cloud architecture

Cloud-native and multi-tier deployments, validated against real breach patterns.

How it works

01

Describe or upload

Paste a system description, or bring existing artefacts — architecture diagrams, API specs.

02

Assets and anti-patterns

The engine extracts and classifies assets, then checks the design against known anti-patterns.

03

Structured threat model

Get an asset inventory and a threat table — categorized, risk-scored, linked to the anti-patterns found.

Who it's for

Security reviewers and individual practitioners — not a heavyweight enterprise platform. It's built for someone who needs a solid first draft of a threat model fast, then applies their own judgment on top. Best used as a structured starting point and gap-identifier, alongside expert review rather than in place of it.
